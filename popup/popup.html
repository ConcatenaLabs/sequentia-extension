Your stakes

The Sequence token (tSEQ) this wallet has staked, from this browser or another wallet with the same phrase. A stake can be unbonded once its lock has passed; it then stops counting as stake, and about two weeks later it can be withdrawn into the wallet.

…

Staking pool

Lend your stake's weight to a pool so it produces blocks for you. Your coins never move and the pool can never spend them: it is lent only the right to sign with your weight. You can take that back at any moment, without asking anyone.

…

Staking new tokens is done from the web wallet, Ambra or the desktop node wallet, and running a pool from the desktop node wallet, which needs a key online on the machine that produces blocks.

Staking rewards

What staking has paid you, in whatever assets the fees were paid in. Sequentia has no block subsidy: a block earns its own transaction fees and nothing else, so rewards arrive in the assets the payers chose. Pool payouts are spendable at once.

…

Once this is on, the wallet sells without asking again. It never converts more than staking has paid you, never touches your stake, and never converts what it cannot get a fair price for — but the selling itself is unattended, which is the point of it.

Leaves

        
Coins held as leaves of an operator's tree: paid and received off the chain, and taken on-chain by this wallet alone when it must. The wallet pins the operator's key and the node's chain when it joins, and refuses any server that names another. The node must validate its anchors and keep a transaction index.

Kept by itself: claims and refresh plans

            
After a sync, at most every ten minutes and once when the wallet opens, in either mode, the wallet takes up every claim named for one of its addresses, signs a refresh plan for every leaf of a round (so a closed wallet keeps it across its expiry, whenever it closes), and cancels the plan of a leaf that moved. A leaf received or boarded out of round is free to pay, and is sent into a round's refresh once that refresh could run within ten days. A planned leaf is paid out of round by no wallet: a payment the unplanned leaves cannot cover gives the planned leaves it needs up in the next round and is made once that round is final, by itself. Release does that for one plan now.

            

            

            

            
          

          

            
The operator's bond

            
The operator's equivocation bond: two messages its key signed under one coin's salt burn it whole. The wallet reads it at care's pace, in either mode, and, once it has seen it bonded, takes no payment out of round while it is missing or in notice, nor more than it is worth. It also watches its evidence against the chain and sends the slash itself should the operator key sign twice.

            

            

            
          

          

            
Issuer-operated assets

            
The operator serves these assets issuer-operated: each round's float is minted by the issuer through its mint limiter, and every batch's sweep can only burn. Supply asks the operator for its report and reads each mint and burn it lists again on this wallet's node; it does not prove the list complete.
